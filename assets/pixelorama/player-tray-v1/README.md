# Eight-direction tray-carrying player

The approved shortened front carrying pose is extended into all eight directions with the same physical left hand, an empty independent circular tray and native 16×24 person scale. No food/items, carrying controls, serving behavior or engine/gameplay logic are included.

Shared frame is 64×32, body offset (24,8), foot anchor (32,31). Source sheet is 320×256: five columns (idle, walk1–4), eight rows (south, west, east, north, northwest, northeast, southwest, southeast). Approved south frames are exact copies translated +20x from their 40×32 review canvas. Timing is 8fps, 500ms idle and 125ms walking.

The native project retains nine semantic actor material roles between two tray depth layers. Near-arm views place the tray in front of the torso and protect the original head where needed; far-arm views place it behind the actor. Separate actor-behind-tray, full tray and actor-in-front-of-tray parts provide one universal reconstruction order. Native tray pixels and all original player sources remain preserved.

Geometry helper produces recipes only. Pixelorama MCP performs all drawing, compositing, native saves and exports through guarded private projects, preserving the shared foreign active canvas.

## Editable source and exports

`player-tray-v1.pxo` contains 40 actual frames, 11 layers, 16 animation tags and the original 17-color palette. Layers 2–10 are the nine actor material layers: contact shadow, shoes, trousers, uniform/cap band, skin, hair, apron/collar, service cap and face/details. First and last layers hold the tray portions behind and in front of the actor. Skin and uniform colors remain separate from shared neutral outlines and cream/chrome colors.

`geometry.json` records the rectangles, fixed anchors and directional depth decisions; `palette.json` records named swatches. `frame-records.json` uses zero-based public indices; Pixelorama tag ranges are one-based. `attachments.json` includes each frame's physical left palm, elbow, tray origin, support point, surface bounds/center and foot anchor. Surface masks describe the original tray top; slots and capacity are unspecified.

Runtime-ready exports are under `apps/web/public/art/player-tray-v1/`. Reconstruct every facing with `actor-behind-tray.png`, `tray-overlay.png`, then `actor-in-front-of-tray.png`, all using the same frame rectangle. Alternatively place the independent 24×16 `tray-body.png` at the frame's tray origin. `actor.png` preserves the complete actor, while `player-with-tray.png` is the reference composite. Nine `material-N.png` atlases preserve complete editable actor materials. Visible skin/uniform masks and per-tone coordinates support recoloring without affecting the tray or other materials. Mask coordinates are frame-local; a role's `layer` is its zero-based actor material index, so the Pixelorama source layer index is `layer + 1`.
